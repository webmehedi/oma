## What to expect

An unfunded launch by one person, honestly:

- Most launches are quiet. A quiet launch is not a failed product — it's a
  distribution problem, and distribution compounds over months.
- Realistic range for day one: `<n>`–`<n>` visitors, `<n>`–`<n>` signups, from
  the channels above. State the assumption behind the range.
- The outcome that actually matters in week one is not signups. It's whether
