# X — day −7 — the one pre-launch post

This is the only row in this calendar that happens **before** day 1, and it is
here because `launch-plan.md` asks for it. It exists to give launch day one
place where people have already seen the thing.

`launch-plan.md` is blunt about the condition: *you cannot manufacture an
audience the week of launch. If you have none here, skip this row rather than
fake it.* That applies to this post specifically.

## Post — attach asset A3

```
Shipping this next week.

It's an invoicing app that can't send invoices, can't take payments, and has two
statuses instead of five.

Everything it doesn't do is so that this bit — type a line item, watch the total
move, save — has nothing in front of it.
```

Fits 280 as written; recount if you edit.

## Asset

**A3** — 10 seconds of the invoice form: type a quantity, type a unit price,
watch the line amount and the total update. No cuts, no music, no captions. The
number moving is the whole asset.
