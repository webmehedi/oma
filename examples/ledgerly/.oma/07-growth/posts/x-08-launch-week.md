## Before you publish

- **`[[TODO: real number]]` is not decoration.** If the real number is 3, post 3.
  If you are not willing to post the real number, delete the sentence — a vague
  "the response has been amazing" is the exact sentence that costs you the
  credibility this whole calendar is built on.
- Do not quote a user unless they said it publicly and you link to it. No
  paraphrased praise, no screenshots of DMs.
- If literally nothing happened and you don't want to say so, skip this row.
  A missing post costs nothing. A fabricated one costs everything.
