# LinkedIn — launch day

The first two lines are everything. On LinkedIn the rest sits behind "see more",
so nothing below line two can rescue a weak opening.

## Post

```
A spreadsheet will give you a number. It won't tell you whether the number is
still true.

That gap is why I spent [[TODO: how long]] building an invoicing app instead of
spending an afternoon fixing the spreadsheet.

It's called Ledgerly, and it is deliberately small. A client list. Invoices made
of line items, with the total computed for me rather than by me. One click to
mark an invoice paid. A dashboard with two figures on it — what's outstanding,
and what came in this month — plus the last ten invoices.

The list of things it refuses to do is longer than the list of things it does.
It does not send email. It does not process payments. It has no draft, sent, or
overdue state — an invoice is unpaid or it's paid, and that's the whole state
machine. There are no charts, because I couldn't think of a chart that answers
"who owes me money" better than the number does.

I know what the objection is: those absences are exactly what the big accounting
suites sell. Fair. But I have used those suites, and the loop I actually run —
make the invoice, get it into a shape I can send, mark it paid when the money
lands — was buried under a hundred features built for a finance department I
don't have.

Built solo. Free right now, because I haven't built billing, not because I've
made any promise about later.

If you invoice clients: [[TODO: URL]]

I'd rather hear what breaks than what's nice. Tell me either way.
```

## Before you publish

- No emoji bullets, no "🚀 excited to share". The first two lines carry this
  post; if you soften them it dies in the feed.
- "[[TODO: how long]]" — if you don't want to say, cut the clause entirely.
  Don't estimate upward.
- If you have a day job and your network includes your employer, decide before
  posting how public you want the freelance side to be. That's a call only you
  can make, and it's easier to make now than after 200 people have seen it.
